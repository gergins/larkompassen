# Lgr22 full coverage programme (grades 1–6)

This is a **coverage ledger**, not a completed curriculum transcription. It lists the 16 core subjects used by the current app in both official grade bands (32 subject-band records). Each is marked **not-inventoried** until its actual central-content headings and items have been checked against Skolverket's official 2025 Lgr22 edition. Subject URLs are lookup references, not evidence of completed review.

Official edition: https://www.skolverket.se/sok-publikationer/publikationsserier/styrdokument/2025/laroplan-for-grundskolan-forskoleklassen-och-fritidshemmet---lgr22?id=13296

## Required completion gates
1. Verify subject applicability per grade band and official source codes; mark subjects not applicable where appropriate.
2. Record every central-content section and item with stable IDs, official grade band, provenance and a human-reviewed paraphrase. Shared SO/NO content for 1–3 must not be duplicated as separate obligations.
3. Include applicable optional/conditional subjects and their eligibility; distinguish them from the 16 core subjects.
4. Map each reviewed requirement to lessons, activities and assessment evidence, or explicitly mark **content missing**.
5. Publish coverage counts with denominators: inventoried requirements, mapped requirements, exercises available and independently reviewed.
6. Distinguish assessment criteria from central content and grade 1–3 vs 4–6; never invent an official weekly or single-year plan.
7. Make any missing/unknown coverage visible in parent UI and verify with tests and independent educational review.

This index is separate from draft PR #7. No merge/deployment approval is implied.
